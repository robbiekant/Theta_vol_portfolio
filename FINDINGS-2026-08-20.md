## 2. The smile, not the level, is where the edge is

* rich **put** wing: SPY −4.06, QQQ −5.17, IWM −4.14, SMH −3.70
* rich **call** wing: **SLV +4.37, GLD +1.57**

Precious-metals upside demand is the one genuinely dislocated wing in this

## 3. Sleeve screen (3 forecasts × mid / execution-adjusted IV)

A name earns a sleeve only if it keeps the same side in every cell.

* **BUY VOL**: GLD (median IV/RV 0.924), SMH (0.897)
* **SELL VOL**: none
* **no edge**: SPY, QQQ, IWM, SLV, ARKK, KWEB, FXI, EWZ

Execution cost is what disqualifies several of them: the ATM straddle's own
bid-to-ask is 5.9 vol points on FXI, 5.2 on ARKK, 4.7 on KWEB. Those are not
tradeable edges, they are spreads.

## 6. Is it a bad regime or a bad idea?

Universe, correlations, forecasts and costs held fixed; only implied vol scales.

| IV scale | income/mo | % NLV | 99% VaR | positions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.00× | $761 | 0.25% | $1,773 | 3 |
| 1.10× | $1,241 | 0.41% | $3,621 | 4 |
| 1.20× | $2,409 | 0.80% | $3,833 | 5 |
| 1.30× | $3,458 | 1.15% | $3,520 | 6 |
| 1.40× | $4,189 | 1.40% | $2,063 | 5 |
| 1.50× | $5,546 | 1.85% | $1,835 | 5 |
| **1.60×** | **$6,037** | **2.01%** | $1,091 | 4 |

The machinery works; today's pricing does not. 2% a month arrives at **1.60×**
today's implied vol — SPY's 29-day ATM near 21% rather than 13.2%, a VIX in the
low twenties. That is a normal volatility regime, just not this one.

Read the *shape* of that curve, not its level: at 1.00× the sweep reprices every
leg from the fitted model rather than from the live bid/ask, so it prints 0.25%
against the live-quote book's 0.20%.

## 7. Correlation — ten tickers is not ten bets

SPY/QQQ 0.93, QQQ/SMH 0.91, KWEB/FXI 0.90, GLD/SLV 0.83. Average absolute
pairwise correlation across the ten-name universe is **0.497** — exactly at the
0.50 book gate. Book Sharpe over single-trade Sharpe is √(N/(1+(N−1)ρ̄)), which
converges to √(1/ρ̄); at ρ̄ = 0.50 the ceiling is 1.41×. Adding a fourth US
equity name buys nothing.

## 8. Measured IBKR IV bias — a correction to a carried-forward figure

IBKR's surface-level `implied_vol_underlying` against the 29-day ATM-forward vol
re-inverted from live mids off the fitted forward: median **+1.57%**, mean
+1.59%, range −0.05% (FXI) to +3.85% (QQQ).

The +6.4% understatement carried forward from the 2026-08-20 ETF feasibility
study does **not** hold on this snapshot. The measured figure is used instead.

## Disclosed limitations

* The return sample is one year for nine of ten ETFs (two years for SPY). That
  is 3–16 *distinct* non-overlapping episodes behind every tail figure, and it
  includes one large equity drawdown.
* Option legs are LIVE-FROZEN, not live: the last regular-session two-sided
  quote, pulled after the 16:00 ET close.
* The vol-shock channel in the VaR is a model, shown at three severities
  (none / base / severe).
* Fills are assumed at mid plus 35% of the half-spread each way, plus $0.65 a
  contract.
* The structure menu is bounded by the 96 legs quoted in this snapshot. A
  production run should quote the full 10–30 delta ladder at both expiries,
  which would widen the credit structures and is the single highest-value
  improvement to make next.
