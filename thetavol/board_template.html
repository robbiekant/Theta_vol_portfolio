Vol points, all of them. front−back is what a calendar earns or pays before anything else happens: positive means the leg you sell is dearer than the leg you buy. RR25 is the 25-delta risk reversal — negative is a rich put wing (equities), positive is a rich call wing (metals). exec cost is the ATM straddle's own bid-to-ask expressed in vol points, so it is measured, not assumed.

2 · Term structure — the structural problem

ATM-forward implied vol at 29 and 57 days, and the forward vol between them
@@term_svg@@
29-day ATM 57-day ATM forward vol, 29→57 (circle) the number above each pair is front−back in vol points
A calendar wants the leg it sells to be dearer than the leg it buys. Nine of ten names offer the opposite. EWZ is extreme at −9.40 vol points: the Brazilian general election sits inside the October expiry, so the back leg carries an event premium the September leg does not. Selling September to buy October there is paying for someone else's event.

Expectancy is measured against each ETF's own historical return distribution, re-centred so E[ST] = F — never a lognormal, which has no skew and no fat tail and so cannot price the only risk a short-premium structure actually carries. Overlapping windows are counted honestly: a year of daily data gives ~7 distinct 29-day episodes, so every tail figure here rests on a handful of real events.

The machinery works; today's pricing does not. Holding everything else fixed, the 2%-a-month target arrives when implied vol sits at @@need@@ today's level — SPY's 29-day ATM at roughly 21% rather than 13.2%, i.e. a VIX in the low twenties. 6 · Correlation — why ten tickers is not ten bets

@@panel_days@@ aligned daily returns
@@corr_head@@@@corr_rows@@
SPY/QQQ 0.93, QQQ/SMH 0.91, KWEB/FXI 0.90, GLD/SLV 0.83. The ten-name universe averages 0.50 absolute pairwise correlation — right at the book gate. Book Sharpe over single-trade Sharpe is √(N/(1+(N−1)ρ̄)), which converges to √(1/ρ̄): at ρ̄ = 0.50 the ceiling is 1.41×, and position ten already captures most of it. Adding names inside the US equity block buys nothing.

Method: Black-76 off a forward fitted per expiration from that expiration's own put-call parity; quadratic smile in log-moneyness with flat wing extrapolation; term structure interpolated in total variance; HAR / 50-50 / 1-year realised-vol forecasts crossed with mid and execution-adjusted implied vol; expectancy against real historical return distributions re-centred to the forward, reported unconditionally and on a calm-regime subsample; managed-exit expectancy from a 5-day block bootstrap; 99% 1-day VaR by historical simulation across the whole book at once, so cross-asset correlation is the realised one.
Disclosed limitations: the return sample is one year for nine of ten ETFs (two for SPY), which is a small number of distinct episodes and includes one large equity drawdown; the vol-shock channel in the VaR is a model, shown at three severities; fills are assumed at mid plus 35% of the half-spread each way.
